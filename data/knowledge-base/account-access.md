# Can't sign in to your Lumora account

If you can't sign in to your Lumora account or the Lumora app, follow these steps.

## Reset your password

1. Go to the sign-in page and select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. Open the reset email and follow the link within **30 minutes**. The link works once.
4. Choose a new password with at least 10 characters.

If the email doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder and make sure you used the
same email address you registered with.

## Account locked

After **5 failed sign-in attempts**, your account is locked for **15 minutes** to protect it.
Wait 15 minutes, then reset your password. Signing in again during the lock restarts the timer.

## Two-step verification codes

If you lost the phone that receives your verification codes, use one of the backup codes you saved
when you turned on two-step verification. Without backup codes, contact support: we verify your
identity with your order number and the last 4 digits of the payment card before removing two-step
verification.

## When to contact support

Contact support if you no longer have access to your account email, or if you see orders or
sign-ins you don't recognise. For suspected account takeover, we lock the account immediately and
reply within **4 hours**.
