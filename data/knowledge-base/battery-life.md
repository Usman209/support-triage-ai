# Battery drains quickly or won't charge

Battery life depends on settings, temperature and battery age. Try these steps first.

## Improve battery life

- Lower screen brightness and screen timeout.
- Turn off features you don't use: GPS, Bluetooth, always-on display, 4K or high-frame-rate video
  on cameras.
- Update the device firmware: updates often include battery fixes.
- Avoid using the device in temperatures below 0 °C or above 35 °C.

## Device won't charge

1. Use the original cable and a charger with the rated power (see the box or the product page).
2. Try a different wall socket and remove any dust from the charging port.
3. Charge for **30 minutes** before turning the device on if the battery was fully empty.
4. Restart the device while it is connected to the charger.

## Battery health and replacement

A battery that holds less than **80% of its original capacity** within the warranty period
(usually **12 months**, or **24 months** with Lumora Care) is replaced free of charge. Swelling,
heat or a burning smell is a safety issue: stop using the device, unplug it and contact support
right away.

## When to contact support

Contact support if the battery lasts less than half of the advertised time after the steps above,
or if the device does not charge with a known-good charger.
