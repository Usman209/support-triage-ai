# Cancel an order or a subscription

## Cancel an order

You can cancel an order free of charge **until it ships**. Go to **My orders**, select the order
and choose **Cancel order**. You get an email confirmation, and the refund starts immediately.

After an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled. Instead, refuse the delivery or return
the item within the return window (see "Request a refund").

## Cancel a subscription (Lumora Care, cloud storage, streaming add-ons)

1. Go to **Account > Subscriptions**.
2. Select the subscription and choose **Cancel subscription**.
3. Your subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period. You are not charged
   again.

If you cancel within **14 days** of the first payment, you get a full refund of that payment.
After 14 days, there are no partial refunds for the current period.

## Annual plans

Annual plans can be cancelled at any time. You keep access until the end of the year you paid
for. Lumora Care annual plans cancelled within 30 days get a refund minus any repairs already
used.

## When to contact support

Contact support if the **Cancel** button is missing, if you were charged after cancelling, or if
you want to cancel an order that is marked "Preparing to ship".
