# Late, missing or damaged delivery

## Delivery times

- **Standard delivery**: 3–5 business days.
- **Express delivery**: 1–2 business days (order before 14:00).
- Large items such as TVs are delivered by appointment; the carrier contacts you to book a slot.

Track your order in **My orders**. Tracking updates can take **24 hours** after the "Shipped" email.

## Order is late

If your order is more than **3 business days** late compared with the estimated date, contact
support. We open an investigation with the carrier, which takes up to **5 business days**. If the
parcel is not found, we send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

## Tracking says delivered, but you don't have it

1. Check with neighbours, building reception and safe places around your door.
2. Wait until the end of the next business day: carriers sometimes mark parcels delivered early.
3. Then contact support within **14 days** of the delivery date.

## Damaged or wrong item

Take photos of the box and the item before you throw away any packaging. Report damage or a wrong
item within **7 days** of delivery. We send a replacement and a prepaid return label; you don't pay
for the return.
