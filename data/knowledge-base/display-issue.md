# Screen flickers, has lines or stays black

## Quick checks

1. Restart the device. For TVs, unplug the power cable for **60 seconds**, then plug it back in.
2. Check cables: reconnect HDMI cables at both ends and try a different HDMI port or cable.
3. Update the firmware or the TV software (Settings > Support > Software update).
4. Turn off energy-saving and auto-brightness features to see if the flicker stops.

## Black screen with sound (TVs and monitors)

Shine a torch close to the screen. If you can see a faint picture, the backlight has failed: this
is a hardware fault. If there is no picture at all, try the TV's built-in menu without any source
connected. If the menu shows, the problem is the source or the cable.

## Lines, dead pixels or a cracked screen

- **Vertical or horizontal lines** that also appear in the TV menu are a panel fault.
- **Dead pixels**: up to **3 dead pixels** are within the manufacturer's tolerance. More than 3,
  or any dead pixel in the centre of the screen, qualifies for a warranty repair.
- **Cracks** are physical damage and are not covered by the standard warranty. Lumora Care covers
  one accidental-damage repair per year.

## When to contact support

Contact support with a photo or short video of the problem and your order number. Panel faults
within warranty are repaired or replaced.
