# Device is broken, overheats or makes noise

## Warranty coverage

- **Standard warranty**: **12 months** from delivery, covers manufacturing faults.
- **Lumora Care**: extends cover to **24 months** and adds one accidental-damage repair per year.
- Not covered: water damage (unless the product is rated waterproof and was used within its
  rating), drops, cracks, and repairs by unauthorised shops.

## Before you request a repair

1. Restart the device and update its firmware.
2. Do a factory reset if the problem continues (back up your data first: a reset erases it).
3. Note when the problem happens and take a short video if you can.

## Overheating

Unplug and stop using a device that is too hot to touch, smells of burning, or makes popping
sounds. These are safety issues: contact support immediately and do not try to charge it again.

## Noisy devices (robot vacuums, fans, projectors)

Clean filters, brushes and vents first. For robot vacuums, remove hair from the main brush and the
wheels; most noise comes from tangled hair.

## How repairs work

We send a prepaid shipping label. Repairs take **7–10 business days** from when we receive the
device. If a device has failed **3 times** for the same fault, we replace it instead of repairing
it again.
