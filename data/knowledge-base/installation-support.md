# Installation help (TVs, thermostats, smart home)

## Wall-mounting a TV

Use a VESA bracket that matches the hole pattern on the back of the TV (see the manual) and is
rated for at least the TV's weight. Mount into studs or solid wall, never into plasterboard alone.
Two people are needed for TVs larger than **55 inches**.

## Smart thermostats

1. **Turn off power to the heating system** at the breaker before removing the old thermostat.
2. Take a photo of the existing wiring and label each wire with the stickers in the box.
3. Check compatibility in the Lumora app **before** installation (see "Product compatibility").
4. If your system has no common wire (C-wire), use the power adapter included in the box.

Systems with line-voltage (high-voltage) heating are not compatible and must not be connected.

## Software installation (apps, printers, accessories)

Download apps and drivers only from the official app store or the manufacturer's website. Install
updates after setup: the first update can take **10–20 minutes**; do not turn the device off.

## Professional installation

Lumora offers professional installation for TVs, thermostats and video doorbells in most areas.
Book it during checkout or in **My orders** up to **14 days** after delivery. Installation by
Lumora technicians is guaranteed for **12 months**.
