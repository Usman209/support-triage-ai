# Device won't connect to Wi-Fi or keeps disconnecting

## Quick fixes

1. Restart the device and your router (unplug the router for **30 seconds**).
2. Move the device closer to the router to rule out weak signal.
3. Check the Wi-Fi password: it is case-sensitive.
4. Update the device firmware and the router firmware.

## 2.4 GHz vs 5 GHz

Many smart-home devices (smart plugs, bulbs, thermostats, robot vacuums) **only support 2.4 GHz
Wi-Fi**. If your router uses one network name for both bands, temporarily turn off 5 GHz during
setup, or create a separate 2.4 GHz network.

## Keeps disconnecting

- Give the device a fixed IP address (DHCP reservation) in your router settings.
- Turn off router features that block smart devices, such as "client isolation" or aggressive
  power-saving.
- Avoid placing the router next to microwaves, cordless phones or thick walls.

## Mesh and business networks

Networks that need a sign-in web page (hotels, offices, some campuses) are not supported by most
smart devices. Use a personal hotspot or a home router instead.

## When to contact support

Contact support if the device can't see any network at all after a restart, or if other devices
connect fine and this one still fails after the steps above.
