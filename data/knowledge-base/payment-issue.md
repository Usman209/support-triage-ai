# Payment declined, charged twice or wrong amount

## Payment declined

- Check the card number, expiry date, security code and billing address.
- Some banks block online or foreign payments: approve the payment in your banking app.
- Prepaid and virtual cards may not support recurring payments for subscriptions.

## Charged twice

A second charge is often a **temporary authorisation hold**, not a real payment. Holds disappear
automatically within **3–5 business days**. If both charges are still there after 5 business days,
contact support with your order number: we refund the duplicate charge within **2 business days**.

For subscriptions, check **Account > Subscriptions** to make sure you don't have two active plans
(for example one monthly and one annual).

## Wrong amount

Prices are confirmed when the order is placed. If an item's price dropped within **14 days** after
your purchase, we refund the difference once (price protection). Currency conversion fees from
your bank are not set by Lumora.

## Invoices

Download invoices in **My orders > Invoice**. Business customers can add a company name and tax
number before checkout; invoices can't be changed after payment.

## When to contact support

Contact support for duplicate charges older than 5 business days, charges you don't recognise, or
a failed subscription payment that keeps retrying.
