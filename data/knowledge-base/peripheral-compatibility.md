# Accessories and peripherals don't work with your device

## Bluetooth accessories (headphones, keyboards, controllers)

1. Put the accessory in pairing mode (usually hold the power or pairing button for **5 seconds**).
2. On the device, remove ("forget") the accessory from the Bluetooth list and pair it again.
3. Keep both within **1 metre** during pairing.
4. Some accessories pair with only one device at a time: disconnect it from your phone first.

## USB and cables

- Use cables that support data, not "charge-only" cables.
- USB-C hubs need enough power: connect the hub's power adapter for monitors and drives.
- HDMI 2.1 features (4K 120 Hz, VRR) need an HDMI 2.1 cable and the right HDMI port on the TV.

## Memory cards (cameras, drones, action cameras)

Use the card speed class listed in the manual (often **V30 or faster** for 4K video). Slower cards
cause recording to stop. Format new cards in the device, not on a computer.

## Third-party accessories

Accessories from other brands may work but are not guaranteed. Damage caused by non-certified
chargers or batteries is not covered by the warranty.

## When to contact support

Contact support if an accessory sold by Lumora as compatible does not work after the steps above.
Accessories bought from Lumora can be returned within the normal **30-day** return window.
