# Check if a product works with your system

## Before you buy

Every product page has a **Compatibility** section. Check:

- **Operating systems**: for example Windows 11, macOS 13 or later, Android 12 or later, iOS 16 or
  later.
- **Smart-home platforms**: Alexa, Google Home, Apple Home, or Matter.
- **Connections**: Wi-Fi band (2.4 GHz / 5 GHz), Bluetooth version, HDMI version.

## Smart-home platforms and Matter

Matter-certified devices work with all major smart-home platforms. Older devices may work with
only one platform: an Alexa-only plug can't be added to Apple Home. Hubs (for example for smart
lights) may be needed for some devices; the product page says "Hub required" when it is.

## Smart thermostats

Use the compatibility checker in the Lumora app: answer questions about your heating system and
upload a photo of the wiring. Most low-voltage (24 V) systems are compatible. Line-voltage
(120 V / 230 V) electric baseboard heaters are **not** compatible.

## Software and games

Software and games list minimum and recommended requirements. Downloaded software can't be
returned once the activation key has been revealed.

## Bought something that isn't compatible?

Unopened products can be returned within **30 days** for a full refund. Opened products can be
returned within **30 days** if they are in like-new condition with all accessories.
