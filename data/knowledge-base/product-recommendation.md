# Get help choosing a product

## Questions that help us recommend

When you ask for a recommendation, tell us:

1. What you will use it for (for example travel vlogs, gaming, video calls, family photos).
2. Your budget range.
3. Devices it must work with (phone, computer, smart-home platform).
4. Size or weight limits (for example a TV for a room where you sit 2.5 metres away).

## Quick guides

- **TV size**: sitting distance in metres × 25 ≈ ideal screen size in inches for 4K. At 2.5 metres,
  a 65-inch TV is a good fit.
- **Action cameras**: choose image stabilisation and waterproofing without a case for sports.
- **Cameras for beginners**: an entry-level mirrorless camera with a kit lens is lighter than a
  DSLR and easier to learn.
- **Robot vacuums**: choose a self-emptying base for homes with pets.
- **Smart lights**: Matter-compatible lights avoid being locked into one platform.

## Price match

Lumora matches the price of major national retailers for the same product, in stock, on the day of
purchase. Price match is not available for marketplace sellers or flash sales.
