# Set up a new device

## Before you start

- Charge the device fully (or for at least **1 hour**).
- Install the **Lumora app** or the manufacturer's app on your phone.
- Have your Wi-Fi name and password ready (most smart-home devices need **2.4 GHz** Wi-Fi).

## Setup steps

1. Turn the device on and wait for the setup light or screen (usually a blinking light).
2. Open the app, select **Add device** and choose the product type.
3. Scan the QR code on the device or in the quick-start guide.
4. Connect the device to Wi-Fi when asked.
5. Install the firmware update offered at the end of setup. Do not turn the device off during the
   update.

## Setup fails

- Restart the device and the app, then try again.
- Reset the device to setup mode (often: hold the reset button for **10 seconds** until the light
  blinks).
- Turn off VPNs and ad blockers on your phone during setup.
- See "Device won't connect to Wi-Fi" for network problems.

## Register your product

Register the product in the app to activate your warranty faster and get safety notices. Your
warranty is valid even without registration, as long as you have proof of purchase.
