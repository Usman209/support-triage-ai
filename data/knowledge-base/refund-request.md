# Request a refund or return an item

## Return window

You can return most items within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund.

- **Unopened** items: full refund.
- **Opened** items in like-new condition with all accessories: full refund.
- **Damaged by use** or missing parts: we may deduct up to **15%**.
- Not returnable: downloaded software with a revealed key, gift cards, and personal-care items
  that have been opened (for example earbuds tips and electric toothbrush heads).

## How to start a return

1. Go to **My orders**, select the item and choose **Return item**.
2. Choose the reason and print the prepaid label (free for faulty, damaged or wrong items).
3. Pack the item with all accessories and drop it at any carrier point within **7 days**.

## When you get your money

We inspect the return within **2 business days** of receiving it. Refunds go back to the original
payment method:

- Cards: **5–7 business days** after inspection.
- Lumora gift balance: immediately.

## Faulty items after 30 days

If the item is faulty after 30 days, it's covered by the warranty instead of a refund (see "Device
is broken"). If a repair is not possible, we replace the item or give a refund.

## When to contact support

Contact support if the refund has not arrived **10 business days** after inspection, or if the
Return button is missing.
