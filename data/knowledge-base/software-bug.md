# App crashes, freezes or behaves strangely

## Quick fixes

1. Update the app and the device firmware to the latest version.
2. Force-close the app and open it again.
3. Restart the device.
4. Clear the app's cache (Settings > Apps > [app] > Storage > Clear cache). This does not delete
   your account or photos.
5. Reinstall the app if the problem continues.

## After a firmware update

If a problem started right after an update, check the **Known issues** page in the Lumora app.
Fixes for known issues are usually released within **14 days**. Rolling back firmware is not
possible on most devices.

## Reporting a bug

Report bugs in the Lumora app (**Help > Report a problem**). Include:

- the device model and firmware version,
- the steps that cause the problem,
- the time it happened (so we can find the logs),
- a screenshot or screen recording.

Turning on **Share diagnostics** in the app sends technical logs only; it does not send photos,
messages or passwords.

## Data safety

If the app shows an error about your account or payment details, don't enter your password again
in a pop-up. Close the app and sign in from the official app or website.

## When to contact support

Contact support if the device can't be used at all, or if a bug causes data loss (see "Recover
lost photos, files or settings").
